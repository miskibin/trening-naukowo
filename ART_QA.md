# Science QA: generated lesson plates

Scope: `android/app/src/main/assets/images/art/`. These are reviewed as simplified conceptual illustrations, not quantitative evidence or a precision anatomy atlas. Verdicts below concern scientific meaning; caption restrictions are part of safe use.

| Plate | Verdict | Scientific check and caption limitation |
|---|---|---|
| `biceps.png` | **Accept as an anatomical overview in supination** | The muscle path, two proximal tendon regions and distal tendon directed toward the proximal radius are plausible. Precise insertions are obscured by overlapping tissue, so do not identify unverified endpoints with callouts. It does not compare pronation and supination. Use as context; the verified source figure and Polish text teach the radial tuberosity insertion. Checked against StatPearls biceps, brachialis and brachioradialis entries listed in the lesson. |
| `movement.png` | **Accept, as a posture comparison** | The two panels plausibly show a flexed elbow with the hand supinated versus pronated. No bones are visible, so it does not show the radius crossing the ulna. Caption: **„Dłoń można obracać przy zgiętym łokciu.”** Do not describe the plate itself as a view of bone movement or biceps attachments. |
| `torque.png` | **Accept, as a load example** | A flexed elbow supports a forearm held horizontally with a dumbbell; gravity acts vertically and creates an external moment about the elbow. The drawing does not mark the force line, perpendicular moment arm, or quantify muscle force. Caption: **„Utrzymanie obciążenia wymaga momentu wokół łokcia.”** Do not claim it measures biceps force or precisely depicts tendon insertions. |
| `sarcomere.png` | **Accept only as a muscle-structure overview** | It suggests bundles, fibers and finer contractile structures, but does not clearly show a sarcomere, Z-lines, actin–myosin sliding, calcium or ATP. ## Verification references

- Adult skeletal muscle fibers have multiple peripheral myonuclei beneath the sarcolemma: [The Myonuclear Domain in Adult Skeletal Muscle Fibres](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9931674/) and [Skeletal Muscle Circulation, ch. 2](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK57140/).
- Leydig cells lie in interstitial tissue between seminiferous tubules; LH from the pituitary stimulates their steroidogenesis: [Endotext: Endocrinology of the Testis and Spermatogenesis](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK279031/) and [StatPearls: Histology, Leydig Cells](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK556007/).
